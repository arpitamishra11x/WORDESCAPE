# 🌍 WordScape

> **Learn words by bringing worlds to life.**

WordScape is an immersive educational experience that transforms early vocabulary learning into a magical adventure. Instead of memorizing words through flashcards, children discover, build, and interact with living worlds where every completed word unlocks a new friend, a new story, and a new memory.

Designed with an Apple-inspired approach to interaction, motion, accessibility, and delight, WordScape combines play, exploration, and education into one seamless experience.

---

## ✨ Vision

Children don't just **spell** words.

They **create** them.

Complete the word **CAT**, and an adorable kitten appears in a beautiful meadow.

Spell **TREE**, and a giant magical tree grows inside your Word Forest.

Every word becomes a living experience that encourages curiosity, imagination, and joyful learning.

# 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Language:** Kotlin
- **UI:** Jetpack Compose
- **Architecture:** MVVM
- **Navigation:** Navigation Compose
- **Animation:** Compose Animation APIs
- **State Management:** ViewModel + StateFlow
- **Dependency Injection:** Hilt
- **Database:** Room
- **Preferences:** DataStore
- **Media:** ExoPlayer / Media3
- **AR (Future):** ARCore
